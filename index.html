Privacy Policy for Mathzoo

Package ID: com.aistudio.mathzoo.kpxzqm • Effective Date: November 26, 2026

100% Offline App No Login Required No Ads or Tracking Family & Child Safe
Quick Summary Mathzoo does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal or sensitive user or device data. Everything in Mathzoo runs locally on your device without an internet connection or user account.

1. Scope & Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Mathzoo handles information when you use the Mathzoo Android mobile application (com.aistudio.mathzoo.kpxzqm). Mathzoo is an offline educational mathematics game app designed for children and students (ages 5–14) and their families.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Mathzoo requires no registration, no login, and no internet connection. We do not collect, access, or transmit:

  • Personal identifiers (such as real names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or account credentials)
  • Precise or approximate location data (GPS, Wi-Fi, or cell tower location)
  • Contacts, calendars, call logs, SMS, photos, videos, or device files
  • Camera images or microphone audio recordings
  • Advertising IDs (AAID), device fingerprints, IP addresses, or usage/analytics telemetry
  • Crash logs sent to external servers or behavioral profiling data

3. Local-Only Data Stored on Your Device

To remember your game progress across sessions, Mathzoo stores limited app data strictly locally on your device inside a private Android Room/SQLite database:

  • Profile & Preferences: Optional child nickname (entered only if you choose to customize it), chosen animal avatar, selected age world (5–6, 7–8, 9–10, 11–12, or 13–14), curriculum board level, and settings toggles (sound effects, voice read-aloud, reduced motion, boss timer, unlock-all).
  • Learning & Reward Progress: Completed games, earned stars, 5-point Reward Points, daily challenge streak, unlocked animal stickers, and topics for extra practice.
Your Control & Deletion: None of this local data is ever uploaded to any server. Parents or users can permanently erase all saved progress anytime by opening Parent Corner & Settings → Reset All Progress inside the app, or by uninstalling Mathzoo from the device.

Note: If you have Android OS device backup enabled in your phone's system settings, Android may include local app state in your private Google account device backup in accordance with Android's standard backup controls.

4. Device Permissions & Offline Features

Mathzoo requests only the minimum permission required for gameplay feedback:

  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used only to provide gentle tactile feedback when tapping answers or interactive math visuals.
  • On-Device Text-to-Speech: If Voice Read-Aloud is enabled, Mathzoo uses Android's built-in system Text-to-Speech engine locally on your device to read questions aloud. No audio is recorded or sent over any network.
  • No Network Permission: Mathzoo does not declare or use the INTERNET permission and does not make network connections.

5. Data Sharing, Third Parties, Ads & Purchases

  • No Data Sharing: We share zero user or device data with any third parties, advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers.
  • No Advertising: Mathzoo contains zero third-party ads or ad SDKs.
  • No In-App Purchases or Subscriptions: All 690 math games and animal stickers are included offline with no payments or external checkout links.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K & Google Play Families)

Mathzoo is built specifically for children ages 5–14 and families. Because Mathzoo operates entirely offline with no accounts, no internet transmission, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs, we do not knowingly collect, use, or disclose personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Parents and guardians retain full control over any optional local nickname and game progress via Parent Corner & Settings.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update Mathzoo in the future in a way that changes how data or permissions are handled, we will update this Privacy Policy and revise the Effective Date at the top of this page prior to releasing the update.
